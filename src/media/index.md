## For producers, editors, and hosts

Jesse has more than three decades of experience as a media host, guest, interviewer, columnist, commentator, and moderator. He is strong live, comfortable in long-form conversation, and useful when a segment needs both intellectual range and conversational momentum.

He is available for podcasts, radio, television, documentary interviews, recurring commentary, panels, and hosted conversations on AI, governance, media power, public trust, disinformation, agriculture, platforms, and systems change.

## Book Jesse as a guest

Jesse works well when the subject is consequential and the format rewards clarity, candour, and synthesis. He can help audiences understand what is structurally changing, what is hype, what institutions are missing, and what questions leaders should be asking next.

Useful formats include live hits, long-form podcast interviews, panel segments, documentary interviews, expert explainers, recurring commentary, and pre-recorded conversations for organizations that need a host who can carry complexity without flattening it.
