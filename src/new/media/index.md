## What this is

Three decades of media work: hosting, interviewing, guesting, moderating, and translating complex material in real time. Live hits, long-form conversation, documentary interviews, and recurring commentary. Comfortable when the segment has to carry both range and momentum.

## Formats

**Live guest.** Breaking news, panel segments, reaction and analysis on short notice.

**Long-form podcast guest.** The format where the argument can actually be developed rather than compressed.

**Host or moderator.** Conversations, panels, and public events where the job is to make other people clear.

**Recurring commentator.** A standing voice for a program or publication covering technology, media, and public trust.

**Documentary interview subject.** For producers who need a subject rather than a pundit.

## What I am useful on

- AI capability claims, governance tradeoffs, and what is actually being deployed
- Disinformation, influence operations, and how public trust is manufactured
- Platform decline, media fragmentation, and AI-mediated information systems
- Institutional legitimacy, and why authority stopped transferring
- Agriculture, food systems, technology adoption, and rural policy
- Agentic computing, and the governance of systems that act

- MyFM Ontario: recurring technology commentary across the network.
