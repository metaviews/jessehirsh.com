## How this becomes work

**Keynote.** A single argument carried through a narrative, built for the room rather than the catalogue. 45 to 60 minutes with real Q&A.

**Workshop.** Decision mapping, governance exercises, and group sensemaking for teams that have to act on this afterwards. 90 to 180 minutes.

**Advisory.** Retained or project counsel on AI adoption, agentic deployment, and governance frameworks, grounded in having built the systems.

**Convening.** Multi-stakeholder roundtables and facilitated sessions, including The Future Herd salon series.
