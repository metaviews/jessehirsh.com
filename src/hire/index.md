Jesse works with organizations in multiple capacities. Not every need fits a keynote slot — and not every engagement is a one-day event.

## Ways to engage

**Strategic advisory**
Retained or project-based counsel for leaders navigating AI adoption, media disruption, or institutional change. Works best for executive teams that need a steady outside perspective grounded in real operational knowledge — not a consultant's slide deck.

**Research and briefings**
Custom research and written briefings on specific questions: emerging risk, policy trajectory, competitive landscape, or technology assessment. Deliverable-driven. Decision-grade. Scoped to what you actually need to know.

**Facilitation**
Multi-stakeholder convenings, retreats, and roundtables. Jesse designs and hosts conversations that move past performance toward actual sensemaking — with diverse interests, conflicting assumptions, and real stakes in the room.

**Workshops and training**
Team-level AI literacy, leadership cohorts, and structured learning experiences built around your organization's context. Hands-on, adapted to your current level of exposure, and built to produce durable capacity — not just awareness.

**Media and commentary**
Available as an expert source, panelist, documentary subject, or recurring commentator for producers covering AI, governance, media systems, and agricultural policy.

---

If none of these categories fit what you have in mind, reach out anyway. The conversation is free.
